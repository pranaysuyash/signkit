The job

A document is not “done” when it opens.

You need to find the right material, prepare it carefully, place it correctly, retain the context, and repeat the work without losing control. SignKit is built around that job, not a single profession or a generic PDF feature list.

Current foundation

A local workspace
for the work in front of you.

Today, the desktop product gives the core job a real home: prepare a source, refine it, keep it in the Vault, and work inside the PDF.

One product model. Three delivery choices.

The work should not force a topology.

Local, Cloud, and Hybrid are not disconnected products. They share the same workflow model, capability taxonomy, audit semantics, and customer-facing truth. What changes is execution and data authority.

Available now

Local execution

The desktop app operates without sync. Documents, signature assets, workflow state, and audit records remain on the customer’s device.

Scale in two directions, carefully

Go deep where the document carries consequence.
Go broad without becoming generic.

Vertical depth

Legal and HR are the proving ground.

Recurring, high-consequence packet workflows are the leading validation wedge. The next question is which exact workflow has the strongest pain, local-first fit, and proof of willingness to pay.

Focus
Recurring packets and reviewed handoffs
Status

Horizontal reach

Build durable surfaces around execution.

Templates, controlled batch work, audit receipts, account operations, and integration adapters can compound value. A generic PDF editor or broad identity suite would dilute the core.

  • Governed templates
  • Controlled batch execution
  • Audit and receipt retention
  • Integration ladder, not integration sprawl

The line to protect

Expansion should make the core more valuable, not less distinct.

01 Do not turn SignKit into a browser PDF editor or a broad DocuSign clone.

02 Do not add sync, integrations, or multi-tenant storage before the capability and data contract is decided.

03 Do not make public claims about future Cloud and Hybrid modes before their proof gates close.
