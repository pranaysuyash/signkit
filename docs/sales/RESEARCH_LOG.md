# SignKit Sales Research Log

## 2026-07-22 — Baseline review

**Evidence tier:** Tier 1 static inspection + Tier 4 public website inspection.

- The live site currently presents SignKit as a privacy-first desktop PDF signature
  toolkit with offline processing, a $29 one-time purchase, and macOS/Windows/Linux
  availability: <https://signkit.work/>.
- The site currently has product screenshots but says customer quotes and case studies
  are still coming soon. This is the first proof-building gap for sales.
- The repository's dated market research prioritizes legal operations, CA/accounting/
  tax practices, and real-estate transaction professionals, with HR and document-service
  teams as secondary segments.
- Existing older GTM projections are historical hypotheses and are not used as verified
  targets in this workspace.
- Search also surfaced a competing/local-PDF positioning signal at
  <https://offlinepdf.app/>. This means “offline PDF” alone is not sufficient
  differentiation; SignKit needs to lead with signature extraction, reusable assets,
  and the specific document workflow it serves.

## Open research questions

- Which segment reports the highest-frequency signature extraction or cleanup pain?
- Is the buyer the practitioner, office manager, or IT/privacy owner?
- Which current tools are used before and after SignKit?
- What proof is required before a team will process a real client document locally?
- Does the $29/$39 one-time offer convert better through self-serve checkout or a guided
  workflow demo?

## Prospect-source policy

The first prospect list will use official company websites, public inquiry pages,
professional directories, and explicit business contact routes. Personal contact data
will not be inferred from email patterns.
