## Evidence and privacy rules

- A prospect is not qualified until the company, workflow, buyer role, and source are
  recorded.
- Use public business information only. Do not collect sensitive personal data.
- Do not infer a person's direct email from a pattern; use a public inquiry route or
  a verified business contact source.
- Record hypotheses as hypotheses. A page view, search result, or plan is not a sale.
- No outreach is sent from this workspace without Pranay reviewing and approving the
  target and message.
- Honor opt-outs and maintain a suppression list before any campaign begins.

## Current scope

The first commercial wedge is privacy-sensitive, document-heavy small teams:

- legal operations and small law firms;
- CA, accounting, and tax practices in India;
- real-estate transaction professionals;
- HR and people-operations teams;
- document-service and back-office operators.

The initial offer is the currently published desktop product. Future regulated
signature, team, or integration capabilities must not be sold as available until the
implementation and legal-review gates are actually closed.
