# SignKit Scaling and Expansion Wayfinder Map

## Destination

Produce a decision-ready, first-principles expansion strategy for SignKit that defines its desktop and web roles, vertical wedges, horizontal modules, integration architecture, commercial packaging, and proof gates. No new product build begins until the map leaves no material product-direction decision unresolved.

## Notes

- Domain: privacy-first local document execution for Legal and HR workflows, with deliberate optional web control-plane expansion.
- Doctrine: `motto_v4.md`, especially one canonical path, durable decisions, the model-pipeline-data separation, privacy/trust boundaries, and evidence-tier honesty.
- This is a planning map. Ticket resolution records decisions; implementation belongs to a subsequent, separately approved execution plan.
- The current evidence inventory is [Scaling and Expansion Evidence Baseline](SIGNKIT_SCALING_EXPANSION_EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md). It distinguishes historical repository ideas, current static implementation evidence, external market evidence, and unverified proposals.
- The [Wide-Open Brainstorm](SIGNKIT_WIDE_OPEN_BRAINSTORM_2026-07-31.md) is a role-separated exploration asset. It informs questions but does not resolve a Wayfinder ticket or establish demand evidence.
- No central issue tracker was provided, so this directory is the Wayfinder local-Markdown fallback. Each child file under `tickets/` is an issue; `blocked_by` records the tracker dependency relationship.
- The `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills referenced by Wayfinder are unavailable in this session. HITL tickets therefore require direct founder/operator discussion and cannot be self-resolved by an agent.
- Research tickets are intentionally unclaimed. The required research-subagent capability is unavailable in this session; they remain ready for an approved research pass.

## Decisions so far

- [Establish the enduring product boundary: local execution and web control plane](tickets/establish-local-execution-web-control-plane-boundary.md) - SignKit will offer Local, Cloud, and Hybrid topologies under one canonical product and workflow model; web can be a full cloud-native product, not only a control plane.

## Not yet specified

- The minimum viable identity, tenancy, and account-recovery model for Cloud and Hybrid is not yet known; it depends on the topology capability/data contract and packaging decisions.
- The precise sync boundary, conflict model, and source-of-truth rules are not yet known; they depend on the topology capability/data contract and integration-boundary decision.
- The right set of regional, industry, and document-system adapters is in scope but cannot be ticketed precisely until the vertical wedge and integration evidence are resolved.
- Public pricing, contract terms, and retention guarantees remain intentionally unspecified until buyer evidence and operational fulfilment are established.
- Whether SignKit should offer a curated, governed template catalog or a broader template marketplace is in scope but cannot be settled before template ownership, capability topology, and legal responsibility are defined.

## Out of scope

- Rebuilding SignKit as a browser PDF editor, cloud document repository, or broad DocuSign clone. Those are different products and would weaken the local execution moat.
- Creating public checkout products, changing production payment settings, or making public pricing claims. These require separate commercial approval after the evidence gates in this map.
- Implementing integrations, API routes, cloud synchronization, or multi-tenant storage while product boundaries are still open.
