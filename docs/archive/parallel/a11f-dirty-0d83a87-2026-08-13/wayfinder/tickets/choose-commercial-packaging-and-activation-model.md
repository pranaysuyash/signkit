# Choose the commercial packaging and activation model

## Question

What should be included in the local core, sold as a module, priced by seat or usage, or reserved for a managed/enterprise offer, and how will a customer receive and activate each entitlement without creating pricing or source-of-truth drift?

## Scope additions

Compare the candidate surfaces in the evidence baseline: Local Core, Local Template and Batch Pack, Automated Packet Ops, Cloud Workspace, Hybrid Workspace, Integration Adapter, and Enterprise Governance. Do not set a public price until validation and fulfilment evidence exists.
