# SignKit experience-system direction

Date: 2026-07-31  
Status: binding product and landing direction before the next parallel concept

## Decision

SignKit's homepage will lead with a job, not a profession, platform label, or
single feature.

The product category is a **local document operations workspace**. Its first
proven workflow is extracting, cleaning, saving, and placing a signature in a
PDF. That workflow is concrete product proof, rather than the permanent limit
of the brand or the information architecture.

The job is:

> Prepare sensitive PDFs correctly, locally, and repeatedly.

Legal, HR, finance, procurement, education, healthcare, independent
professionals, and individuals may recognize themselves in examples later in
the page. None becomes the hero's exclusive audience.

## Why this replaces the former brief

The former brief incorrectly narrowed the entry point by asking for a primary
visitor. That treats a shared document-preparation job as a persona problem and
would force the landing page to be redesigned whenever the product grows.

Signature extraction is a useful starting product surface. Vault, document
cleanup, templates, batch work, controlled workflows, review, audit history,
AI assistance, and integrations all fit naturally inside the document-operations
workspace frame.

## Experience-system principles

One brand system, with distinct capability expressions:

| Surface | Desired character | Evidence rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Cinematic, confident, spatially calm | A real current product workflow, not a fictional document or fake signature interaction |
| Workspace | Precise, dense, tool-like | Current desktop capture and real controls |
| Vault | Quiet, archival, owned | Current vault evidence only, no invented stored documents |
| Automation | Alive, connected, inspectable | Shipped workflow evidence, otherwise explicitly labelled exploration |
| AI | Adaptive and assistive | Never imply autonomous accuracy or a capability that is not current |
| Trust | Near-invisible evidence | Local processing, performance, and product facts, not decorative security theatre |

The variation is intentional. It must remain legible as one SignKit system
through shared typography, colour roles, spacing, interaction timing, and
accessibility rules.

## Landing composition for the next parallel concept

1. **Job-led opening**: "Prepare sensitive PDFs, locally." The current
   extraction and placement flow proves that claim.
2. **Real-workflow proof**: selection, cleanup, library, and placement shown as
   real desktop states, sequenced with restrained motion rather than simulated
   controls.
3. **Workspace breadth**: explain the surrounding document workspace as
   capabilities, not a pile of generic feature cards.
4. **Role recognition**: later examples for different document-heavy contexts,
   each tied to the same job rather than splitting the site into persona pages.
5. **Expansion without fiction**: distinguish current capability, emerging
   controlled workflows, and research directions. Future product visuals must
   be abstract system diagrams or clearly identified explorations, never a fake
   shipped dashboard.
6. **Trust as evidence**: local-first behavior and customer-relevant product
   proof, with no legal-signing, consent, ownership, or guarantee claims that
   the product does not establish.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

- No fake consent sheets, generic signatures, or theatrical extraction sliders.
- No persona-led hero or "Mac utility" positioning.
- No made-up workflow screen treated as current product proof.
- No uniform visual treatment forced across workspace, vault, automation, AI,
  and trust.
- No claim that certificate signing, cloud collaboration, or autonomous AI work
  is presently shipped without runtime evidence.

## Evidence and next action

Current mac-premium surfaces are captured in
`Docs/review/assets/current-premium-product-surfaces-20260731/`. The next
concept must use those captures and a fresh end-to-end loaded-PDF placement
capture as product evidence.

The experience system must also express the canonical Local, Cloud, and Hybrid
delivery model. Local execution is current product proof; Cloud and Hybrid are
status-labelled direction until their capability/data contract and proof gates
are closed. The topology-led parallel application is
`web/concepts/2026-07-31-topology-experience/` and its evidence review is

The rejected `web/concepts/2026-07-31-b2c-redesign/` concept is preserved as a
non-eligible exploration. The replacement will be a separate parallel concept,
not an incremental reskin.

## Anything else?

Yes. The durable output is an experience system, not merely a home-page mockup.
It should establish reusable page archetypes, product-demo conventions, motion
rules, visual evidence rules, accessibility behavior, and performance budgets
for future SignKit surfaces.

## Addendum (2026-07-31): Product Museum information architecture

This record is superseded on experience architecture by
[`2026-07-31_product_museum_experience_architecture.md`](../decisions/2026-07-31_product_museum_experience_architecture.md).

The corrected interpretation of Linear and Raycast is not "use their visual
style". It is that SignKit must make a visitor understand the product through
an operable, causal product experience before the supporting copy explains it.
The landing is a guided product journey, not a set of screenshots with labels.

The old statement that the hero must always be a literal current capture was
too rigid. Current runtime captures remain the source of truth for **current
product proof**. An explicitly labelled illustration or interaction study is
also valid for **future-interface direction**. It must never be represented as
a shipped interface or used to imply an unshipped capability.

The later artefact `web/concepts/2026-07-31-workbench-experience/` is therefore
a visual-direction study only. Its static generated workbench does not meet the
new living-UI requirement. The next concept must demonstrate the causal flow
from source selection through refinement and PDF placement with semantic,
keyboard-operable UI states.
