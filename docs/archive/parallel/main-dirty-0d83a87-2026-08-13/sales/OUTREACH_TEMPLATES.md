# SignKit Outreach Templates

These are starting points, not send-ready bulk campaigns. Each message needs a real
workflow observation and must be reviewed before sending.

## Discovery-first email

**Subject:** Question about your PDF/signature workflow

Hi [Name],

I’m researching how [type of team] handles [specific document workflow]. SignKit is a
small desktop tool for extracting reusable signatures and completing PDFs locally, so
confidential working files do not need to be uploaded to a signing service.

I noticed [specific public evidence]. Is [pain hypothesis] a real issue for your team,
or do you already have it solved?

If useful, I can show the workflow in five minutes. If this is not relevant, I will not
follow up.

— Pranay

## Value-led follow-up

Hi [Name],

One detail that may be relevant: SignKit is designed for local PDF preparation and
signature reuse, with a one-time desktop purchase rather than a monthly plan. It is not
a replacement for regulated signing or a full agreement-management system.

Would a short look at [specific workflow] be useful?

## Partner/integration conversation

**Subject:** Local PDF preparation for [company/workflow]

Hi [Name],

I’m exploring whether SignKit could complement [company's existing workflow] by handling
local signature extraction, cleanup, and PDF preparation before files enter the system
of record. I’m looking for workflow feedback first, not asking you to change platforms.

Would you be open to a short conversation about where local preparation is currently
awkward or costly?

## Opt-out acknowledgement

Thanks for letting me know. I’ve recorded the opt-out and will not contact you again
about SignKit.

## Personalization checklist

- Name and role are verified from a public business source.
- The workflow observation is specific and non-sensitive.
- No unsupported legal, security, savings, or competitor claim is included.
- The message asks one low-friction question.
- The recipient has a clear opt-out path.
