## How to read this batch

These are organizations with public evidence of legal/document-heavy work and an
explicit business contact route. The fit score is a hypothesis using the repository's
five-dimension rubric; it is not a statement that the organization has confirmed a
SignKit need. No personal email was inferred, and no outreach has been sent.
